## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Hiring engineers and recruiters with about 30 seconds.** They land on the demo page from a CV, a README or a
   message. Their job: understand the problem, the finding and why the evidence is credible, then decide whether to
   look further.
2. **Engineers who dig in.** They want the evidence behind each number (the source file, row and commit), the recorded
   heals and traces, and the code. They will open the replay, the reports and the repository.

## Product Purpose

Nexus is a reliability layer for LLM agents. It detects agent failures (loops, tool misfires, context bleed, plan
drift, hallucination) from traces, lets an LLM critic propose a fix, and promotes a fix only if it passes a three-check
gate: it repairs the failing task, it generalises to held-out tasks, and it breaks nothing that already passed (a
regression set). Open source; the Python SDK is `nexus-llmops`.

This surface (`dashboard/`, replay mode) is the public demo: a static, read-only replay of recorded DEV evidence with
no backend, keys or model calls. Success: a reader leaves knowing the problem, the finding and that every number is
traceable to a committed file.

## Positioning

The claim is about verification, not generation: an LLM can write plausible fixes, and the measured finding is that a
third of the critic's genuine patches (12 of 36 other_fix patches on the sealed TEST split) broke a task that already
passed. The common "rerun improved" promotion rule would have promoted patches the gate rejected (6 false promotions).
Nexus's gate checks the failing task, held-out tasks and previously passing tasks before promoting.

## Operating Context

- Readers arrive cold, often on a phone or a laptop between other tabs.
- The evidence lives in the repository: `RESULTS.md` (every result row with 95% intervals), `results/*.json`,
  `results/phase4/headline_transcribed.json` (the Phase 4 headline transcribed from the sealed TEST report),
  `reports/phase6/*.md`.
- The page reads every number from `dashboard/public/replay.json`, generated by `dashboard/scripts/export_replay.py`
  from committed results and checked in CI (`tests/unit/collector/test_replay_export.py`).
- The replay build is offline: no request leaves the host (web fonts are stripped from the replay HTML).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Detection: scored Phase 2 detectors; the live classifier reproduces their DEV predictions on all 359 DEV traces.
- Healing: an LLM critic proposes a patch; the gate runs repair, held-out and regression checks; recorded Phase 5
  overnight DEV runs back the replay.
- Every number on the page comes from `replay.json` at render time; words that encode a number are computed from it.
- The NexusBench TEST split is sealed. TEST numbers appear only as already reported (scored once) and are labelled TEST.
- Wording: "false promotions", never "harmful". "The gate blocked all 12" is derived from the gate definition, not a
  measured result, and must not be claimed as one.
- Install command shown: `pip install nexus-llmops` (decided 2026-11-13; the page ships to production only after 0.1.0
  is on PyPI).
- No stock photography or decorative imagery. Visuals are typography, diagrams, numbers and the heal walkthrough.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Nexus. Package: `nexus-llmops`. Repository: github.com/sankalp-rajeev/Nexus.
- Voice: a careful research write-up crossed with good developer docs. Confident, plain, evidence-first, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Phase 4 sealed TEST finding (`results/phase4/headline_transcribed.json`): gate promoted 25/51 (critic) vs 12/51
  (generic) vs 0/51 (no patch); 12/36 other_fix patches broke a passing task; old rule promoted 32/48 with 6 false
  promotions; 0/25 gate-promoted patches failed on fresh seeds.
- Detection: TEST macro recall 0.9556 vs 0.2889 for the shipped SVM baseline (`RESULTS.md` D1, D2); HALLUCINATION
  precision 0.811 missed its 0.90 target, and the page should not hide that.
- Nine recorded DEV heals and 21 DEV traces in `replay.json`.
- Absent: users, customers, testimonials, logos, production deployments, PyPI 0.1.0 release. Never fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before claims: every number carries its source, scope (DEV or TEST) and denominator.
2. Report misses as plainly as wins.
3. Say exactly what was measured; derived statements are labelled as derived.
4. The 30-second reader gets the problem, the finding and the credibility; the deep reader gets the files.
